# **AssetWitness**

### **Every handover, verified.**

A Property Technology venture proposed for Sustainnovation 2026, built on the AI evidence engine originally developed for RoomWitness (BDI Bangkok Hackathon 2026).

---

## **Executive Summary**

AssetWitness turns a photo walkthrough of any space, government office, retail unit, or co-working suite, plus the relevant lease or occupancy agreement, into a certified, dated condition record in minutes. It is derived from RoomWitness's proven 4-agent AI pipeline, but repositioned entirely: instead of helping an individual tenant build a case against a landlord, AssetWitness gives DAD (Dhanarak Asset Development Co., Ltd.) a neutral system of record for every handover across its property portfolio, protecting asset value, speeding up occupancy turnover, and giving every tenant, government agency or commercial operator, a fast and transparent process. The long-term ambition is for AssetWitness to become the standard handover-certification layer not just for DAD, but for the wider network of Thai state enterprises that manage real estate.

## **The Opportunity: DAD Is Mid-Rollout, Right Now**

DAD's own flagship initiative, publicly named "Road to Sustainnovation," is actively underway:

* Building C and the Pod Duang building together total roughly 510,000 square meters of state property.  
* As of early 2026, 17 of a planned 25 government agencies had already moved in, with 2 more expected during 2026 and full occupancy targeted for 2027\.  
* Beyond office space, the Pod Duang building alone carries around 29,754 square meters of commercial space (floors 1 to 3: shops, restaurants, banking, services), over 5,000 square meters of co-working and serviced office space (floors 4 to 5), further commercial space on floor 6, and a 2,000-plus square meter conference and event floor on floor 7, the same floor where Sustainnovation's own bootcamp is held.  
* An earlier commercial leasing program across the older Government Complex buildings (A and B) already lists as many as 262 individual shop units.

This means DAD is not a hypothetical future customer. It is actively onboarding new government tenants on a hard 2027 deadline, and it is simultaneously running a large, constantly turning-over commercial tenancy across multiple buildings. Every one of those transitions, agency move-ins, shop lease renewals, co-working turnover, needs its condition documented at handover. That volume, on that timeline, is the opening AssetWitness is built for.

## **The Problem**

At this scale, a handover record kept on paper or in scattered photos does not hold up. Whether it is a government agency taking over a newly fitted-out floor, a restaurant operator ending a lease in Building C, or a co-working tenant vacating a serviced suite, someone needs to answer three questions quickly and defensibly: what condition was this space in when it changed hands, what changed since, and who is responsible for the difference. Without a consistent, timestamped, portfolio-wide answer to that, DAD absorbs slower turnarounds between tenants, harder-to-resolve disputes over damage or incomplete fit-out, and no aggregate view of the condition of assets it is accountable for. That gap gets more expensive, not less, as the portfolio keeps growing toward the 2027 target and beyond.

(We're treating this as a strong, well-grounded hypothesis rather than a confirmed fact. It is the honest problem worth validating directly with DAD's asset management team.)

## **The Solution**

AssetWitness takes the same core capability RoomWitness proved out (turning photos and a contract into a structured, cited, ready-to-use document) and re-points it at DAD as the asset owner rather than at an individual renter. The system is deliberately neutral: it protects DAD's asset value and it gives every tenant, whether a government agency or a shop operator, a fair and fast process. That neutrality is the actual product.

### **How it works**

1. **Condition Comparison Agent**: compares handover photos or video against the space's prior condition record, mapping any change.  
2. **Agreement Parser Agent**: reads the lease, occupancy agreement, or fit-out contract to establish what each party is responsible for.  
3. **Asset Policy Reasoning Agent**: applies DAD's own occupancy and maintenance policies (rather than consumer deposit law) to classify each change as normal wear, tenant responsibility, or DAD's responsibility.  
4. **Report Generation Agent**: produces the actual documents DAD's team needs, a condition certification report, a fit-out completion checklist, or a liability summary if a dispute arises.

### **Product line**

* **Handover Certification** (core product): condition documentation at move-in and move-out for any space type.  
* **Portfolio Condition Dashboard**: an aggregated view across every building DAD manages, surfacing where deferred maintenance or high-dispute patterns are concentrated, feeding directly into asset-value decisions.  
* **Dispute Resolution Support**: structured, evidence-backed documentation for the cases that do escalate, whether between DAD and a commercial tenant or between DAD and a contractor over fit-out completion.

## **Target Customers**

* **Primary buyer**: DAD's asset and leasing management teams.  
* **End users**: incoming government agencies' facilities staff (the remaining agencies due by 2027), commercial tenants across DAD's retail, dining, and co-working spaces, and DAD's own property operations staff.  
* **Expansion buyers**: other Thai state enterprises and ministries that manage their own real estate portfolios, once AssetWitness has a proven track record inside DAD.

## **Theme and Strategic Alignment**

**Theme: Property Technology (PropTech).** The theme's own definition calls for applying technology to real estate, buildings, and smart cities to improve management efficiency, which is exactly what this is.

* **SO2 (create good experience and shared value for customers and stakeholders): primary.** A fast, transparent, fair handover process is a direct, tangible improvement in experience for every agency and tenant DAD works with, and it matches DAD's own public framing of Building C as a modern, lifestyle-forward workplace.  
* **SO1 (develop state assets for added economic value): secondary.** Faster turnaround between tenants, fewer unresolved disputes, and a portfolio-wide condition record all protect and grow the value of the assets DAD is accountable for.  
* **SO3 (BCG model building management): a smaller, honest claim.** The condition data AssetWitness collects could eventually support material reuse and circularity tracking during fit-outs and renovations, worth mentioning as a future direction rather than a current strength.

## **Business Model**

* **Platform license fee**, charged per building or per portfolio, paid by the asset-holding organization (DAD initially).  
* **Per-handover processing fee**, scaled to transaction volume, which grows naturally as the remaining agencies move in through 2027 and as commercial leases continue turning over.  
* **Portfolio Dashboard** as a higher tier, priced for asset managers who want aggregate visibility rather than per-transaction detail.

Exact pricing needs validation with DAD directly. RoomWitness's own unit economics (AI cost of roughly 15 to 50 baht per case, high gross margin at volume) suggest the underlying cost structure carries over well to an enterprise license model, since the same pipeline is doing the work.

## **Go-to-Market**

1. **Phase 1, Pilot**: embed AssetWitness in the live handover wave already happening at Building C and Pod Duang, the remaining agencies moving in through 2026 and 2027\. This is a real, current, time-bound opportunity, not a hypothetical rollout.  
2. **Phase 2, Portfolio-wide**: extend to DAD's other managed buildings, including the older Government Complex buildings A and B and their existing commercial tenant base.  
3. **Phase 3, Beyond DAD**: license AssetWitness to other Thai state enterprises and ministries managing their own real estate, positioning it as shared national infrastructure for state-asset handover documentation rather than a single-client tool.

## **Competitive Landscape**

* **Manual, paper-based handover checklists**: presumed current default, slow and hard to aggregate across a growing portfolio.  
* **Generic international PropTech platforms**: not built for Thai state-asset regulation, procurement rules, or bilingual documentation needs.  
* **Nobody currently serves this exact niche**: a handover and condition-certification layer purpose-built for Thai government property management.
